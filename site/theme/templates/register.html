Ground Rules

When using this platform, please keep several ground rules in mind!

Writeups

The challenges created for this platform are educational material. Because of this, we would appreciate that writeups, walkthrough videos, and livestreams of challenge solutions are not posted to the internet. Obviously, we can't stop you from posting things to the internet, but we worked hard to make all of this public, and we would appreciate your help in keeping Codesafe a viable educational platform.

Reporting Bugs

This platform is still under active development! If you find a bug or even a vulnerability in this platform, please contact us.

Active Research & Development

The challenges, platform, and even infrastructure behind Codesafe is always changing and evolving, and at this time, Codesafe is not considered to be a stable live service. With regards to registration, this means that your challenge progress may NOT be saved and your accounts could be wiped (especially for our first few field tests as we create a stable process).

You do not have to use a valid email at this time, and we recommend not using a password that you use for other accounts. We do recommend picking a username that can be identified to you or easily remembered, especially if your participation can go towards extra credit.
